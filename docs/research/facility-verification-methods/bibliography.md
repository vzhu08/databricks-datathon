# Primary sources · Cycle 01 · November 22, 2026

Dates below are publication or edition dates where available. Undated documentation is cited as consulted on November 22, 2026 ET. The memo cites project-owned first-hand experiments separately, with their measured denominators. Documentation about a programme or dataset supports the stated field interpretation. The applicable license and project permissions must be checked for each edition actually used.

## Address and linkage

- **B1. Fellegi and Sunter, 1969.** The original model defines linkage decisions, including a clerical-review region, by comparing agreement patterns of matches and nonmatches. It motivates graded comparisons and abstention. It supplies no India hospital benchmark. [A Theory for Record Linkage](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1969.10501049)
- **B2. Madhani et al., December 2023.** The authors report 26 million training/resource transliteration pairs across 21 Indic languages and 12 scripts, and a 103k-pair test set over 19 languages. Word transliteration is the measured task. Pin the exact model and review its repository and artifact licenses if trialled on records. [Aksharantar, Findings of EMNLP](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.4/) · [IndicXlit release and licensing](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicXlit)
- **B3. Department of Posts, current OGD catalogue, and Ministry of Communications, May 27, 2025.** The PIN directory supplies postal context. The later announcement introduces a georeferenced PIN boundary dataset as a separate edition. An office or area still leaves civic address and hospital building unresolved. Keep the project comparison on the approved v0 source. [PIN directory](https://data.gov.in/catalog/all-india-pincode-directory-through-webservice) · [PIN boundary announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2131707&lang=2&reg=3)

## Identifiers and change

- **B4. WHO, 2018.** The Master Facility List guidance defines minimum identifying and service data, gives an identifier and maintenance design, and discusses governance for changes. Each country implements its own master list and change policies. WHO's guide specifies its publication license. [Master Facility List Resource Package](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/-9789241513302)
- **B5. National Health Authority, undated.** HFR registration generates a Facility ID. The HEM guide explicitly describes linking that ID in the administrative portal. This is a documented bridge mechanism. Its coverage, edit history and permitted bulk transfer are unresolved for this project. Use Vincent's approved access and obtain facility-only fields. [HFR–HEM linkage FAQ and linked manual](https://supportbeta.nha.gov.in/kb/faq.php?id=86)
- **B6. US Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, July 2026 data dictionary.** An example of explicit administrative event grain and separate buyer, seller, provider and effective-date fields. Its jurisdiction is the US Medicare hospital population. It illustrates why ownership, registration and physical site continuity should be represented separately. [Hospital Change of Ownership Data Dictionary](https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/Hospital_CHOW_Data_Dictionary.pdf)

## Geometry

- **B7. OpenStreetMap Foundation, current documentation.** OSM data uses ODbL with attribution and applicable share-alike obligations. Database, imagery and tile terms need separate checks. OSM's entrance tag describes an access point. The absence of a tag leaves the entrance unknown. [Copyright and license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [OSM entrance tagging reference](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:entrance)
- **B8. Overture Maps Foundation, current schema.** The SourceItem dictionary identifies contributing datasets and allows record/property attribution. Check source per assertion. A feature identifier alone does not demonstrate independent survey or a current premises. Use the license for the actual release and inputs. [SourceItem schema](https://docs.overturemaps.org/schema/reference/common/source_item/)

## Service and equipment

- **B9. Ministry of Health and Family Welfare/NHA, June 6, 2022.** The PM-JAY dashboard announcement distinguishes national/state aggregates and treatment counts from authorized amounts. Facility-only material would require a separate approved route. This study uses no individual encounters. [PIB public dashboard announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1831575&lang=2&reg=48)
- **B10. Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, HMIS User Guidelines version 1.5.** Facility reporting and reporting-period workflow illustrate denominator and submission grain. Inspect indicator definitions, completeness and the relevant state/edition scope before interpreting a procedure aggregate. A reporting system's existence supplies no reuse grant or facility export. [HMIS User Guidelines](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/7216231442HMISUserGuidelines.pdf)
- **B11. Ministry of Labour and Employment/ESIC, March 24, 2025; PIB, December 13, 2020.** AU3910 supplies labelled hospital/service entries and an answer date. The Sanath Nagar dedication release separately dates a named branch event to December 12, 2020. Both are operator-origin claims. Project access and sharing are governed by the existing #232 disposition. [Lok Sabha AU3910 annexure](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/184/AU3910_nUKW7U.pdf?source=pqals) · [PIB dedication release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1680350&lang=2&reg=48) · [Project disposition](https://github.com/vzhu08/databricks-datathon/issues/232#issuecomment-7076281929)
- **B12. NHSRC, January 2025 document path.** Maintenance, calibration and testing guidance informs separation of commissioning, functional checks and service use. The historical inventory's row grain and access are established in the project receipts. [Guidelines on Equipment Maintenance, Calibration and Testing](https://nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2025-01/Guidelines%20on%20Equipment%20Maintenance%2C%20Calibration%20and%20Testing.pdf) · [Pinned NHSRC site linkage](https://github.com/vzhu08/databricks-datathon/issues/208#issuecomment-7075070242)
- **B13. NHSRC, August 2023 file edition.** The draft model maintenance RFP is a template for operational responsibilities and equipment records. A signed site-specific record would require its own provenance, date and reuse scope. [Draft model RFP](https://nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2023-08/Revised%20Draft%20Model%20RFP%20BMMP.pdf) · [BEMMP programme documentation](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/biomedical-equipment-management-and-maintenance-program-bemmp)

## Access and rights

- **B14. Government of India, 2017 open-data license; Parliament of India, 2005 Right to Information Act.** The open-data license has attribution requirements, conditions and exclusions. Apply it to an identified licensed release. The Act supplies a formal route to request existing records from eligible public authorities, subject to statutory scope and exemptions. It does not resolve permission to redistribute a particular result. No request was drafted for sending or submitted in this cycle. [GODL–India](https://data.gov.in/Godl) · [Right to Information Act, 2005](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/17520?col=123456789%2F2510&view_type=search)
- **B15. NABH/QCI, programme qualification verified by Vincent November 22, 2026.** The approved subset has accreditation entries and PDF-path locators with no cleared dated service field. The narrow paper request uses the issuer Secretariat and asks for branch address/ID, exact scope, interval and original locator under explicit four-reader permission. Check current terms and written scope with Vincent. [Qualification and permission dependency](https://github.com/vzhu08/databricks-datathon/issues/221#issuecomment-7076246901) · [NABH publisher terms](https://nabh.co/terms-and-conditions/)
- **B16. Common Crawl, current service terms, and project proposal #217, crawl CC-MAIN-2026-39.** Service access is subject to Common Crawl terms and underlying content can have separate rights. The capture dates observation of text. Use the original page's event date and branch context for clinical claims. [Common Crawl terms of use](https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use) · [Project sampling and findings](https://github.com/vzhu08/databricks-datathon/pull/217)
