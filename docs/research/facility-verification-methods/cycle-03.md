## Proposed comparison and stop

1. **Reproduce Town-first on six frozen source rows.** Preserve the one historical witness, the five unqualified rows and the absence of same-city siblings. Separately retain the 24 immutable VF occurrence slots (12 missed, 12 competing) and their 0/24 approved-query result. Do not resample either denominator to improve apparent coverage.
2. **Reverse lookup from the strongest registry first, if a source gate passes.** Within one exact approved registry edition, count all rows, usable public label/locator, dated branch-specific premises (effective date/validity or explicit historical observation), exact civic or named-premises identifier, district/subdistrict and missing Town separately. Require evidence that a date actually applies to that premises. Date on a snapshot or roster footer is insufficient for individual validity. Freeze up to eight new dated public premises with missing-Town NHP counterparts, using stable issuer-key order and keeping every failed slot. Include up to four documented same-city sibling challenges where available and disclose the count. Join each to NHP by normalized branch name, official district or subdistrict role and an explicit civic or named premises match. Check all same-name sites in the entire available district universe and compatible rivals beyond district boundaries if locality changed. Civic/unit numbers and conflicting addresses are vetoes. PIN is supporting context. Uniqueness within a partial source index remains provisional. When a key or effective date is absent, count the row in the first failed gate and publish nonexclusive missing-field counts to expose overlap. A refreshed snapshot repeating the same source record, same-TPA copies and official NHP versus its mirror each count once. Different publishers can still have one origin.
3. **Validate gains against two separate denominators.** Count new independently adjudicated public branch cases over the frozen registry candidate slots, and qualify/24 on the frozen VF frame with counts by misses and competing cases. For paper false positives use same-city institutional siblings, reused ROHINI, copied publisher rows and a historical relocation with identical name. Two reviewers check the candidate and competing premises from permitted evidence. One false branch acceptance freezes promotion. Give search providers only literal wording cleared from public records. If testing is later authorized, use an actual new Google India observation on every same query and spelling arm, with paired access and adjudication counts as in Cycle 02. No benchmark claim enters the VF cohort without an independently reviewed local link. Count licence-denied, missing-date, vague-premises, unverified lineage and unresolved-rival rows rather than dropping them.

The reverse design may qualify additional public benchmark cases, with **unmeasured marginal yield** on both denominators. It needs at least one permitted dated premises record with independently attributable evidence and a usable district/subdistrict/civic crosswalk beyond the existing witness before starting. The marginal VF payoff of resolving the one pending join is bounded at **one additional eligible query out of 24**, conditional on a dated discriminator, clearance and no viable rival. A single permitted branch-level response targets that exact failure with less review than an edition-wide reverse comparison. Anticipated effort is one pass on the single case versus one registry field-count pass and up to eight reviewed joins plus controls for the broader design. Actual response latency, cost and verification yield are unknown.

There is currently no measured newly eligible independent witness in these receipts. Defer further lookups and replays. Reopen the one candidate if Vincent obtains a dated issuer branch ID or a dated full civic/unit address that distinguishes it from the sibling premises. Reopen the benchmark only on verified additional public branch witnesses, or if J's completed geometry supplies an independent premise discriminator that changes eligibility. National totals remain 10,088 occurrences and 30,264 service decisions.
