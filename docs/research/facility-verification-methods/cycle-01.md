# Facility verification methods · Cycle 01 · November 22, 2026

## Decision

Keep the issuer-first approach and test two ways to recover information that the frozen comparisons lack. **First, test whether script-aware address retrieval and an explicit coordinate-uncertainty rule can recover a uniquely identifiable premises without accepting a neighboring branch. Second, test whether dated issuer records can place a specific service at that premises in the same period.** Vincent (B) owns source permissions and joins, D can reproduce the comparisons, and Oliver decides whether to expand. Both are bounded research experiments using retained inputs subject to their existing access scopes.

When neither address nor independently sourced geometry can distinguish branches, the highest-value location field is a publisher-attributed physical branch ID with address and dated changes. When location is clear but service is uncertain, the highest-value service record is a dated, branch-keyed aggregate of completed procedures or explicit onsite operating evidence, with units and onsite/PPP/referral scope. A dated equipment acceptance record with a delivery site and a separate operating event is the next useful combination for MRI. Request these fields through Vincent if a later cycle authorizes outreach.

Keep the national frame of 10,088 original VF occurrences, 10,077 upstream IDs, 11 identical pairs and 30,264 original decisions. Assess distinct premises and deduplicate payloads for reliability estimates while reporting gains at both premises and occurrence grain. Keep location, historical activity, and present operational evidence as separate outcomes. Neither experiment proposes a change to a routing input. The team's [service evidence rules](../../implementation/SERVICE_STATUS.md) govern adjudication.

## Pairing and acceptance

Use address or stable source ID to generate candidates, named premises evidence to distinguish branches, and dated activity to establish service at that site. An address and a point generated from that address are complementary fields from one source. Evidence of an entrance can improve route precision independently of service. A machine record and a completed procedure log can answer different questions when both name the host in overlapping periods. Disagreement between periods can be a change rather than a contradiction. Evaluate both on an explicit date or date interval.

Count origins at the assertion level. Collapse a publisher's releases of the same return, VF website extracts and their archived copies, HOT/PBF derivatives, shared Overture imports, and an accreditation map link copied from the same map. Check the upstream record ID and capture/observation history before treating voices as distinct. Correlation unknown means no independent corroboration credit. Never compute a multi-source score using the number of URLs.

## Two experiments for Vincent and Oliver

### A. Script-aware retrieval followed by a coordinate uncertainty gate

**Question.** Can an extra retrieval route expose missed branch candidates, then use independently originated premises evidence to rule out all known rivals? #235 tested fixed address normalization and #234 tests map premises coverage. The new comparisons are transliteration as a retrieval-only arm and sensitivity to uncertainty with a required margin over rival premises. Compare all 49 #221 listings to all 10,088 originals. Five listings lack an address key or linked point. Reviewers see only the scope needed for the task. Use already approved #221, VF and #111/#234 inputs. Vincent decides whether any cross-lane read is allowed. Use the pinned IndicXlit release only with sufficient authorized native-script fields. No-script rows remain in denominator.

| Arm | Frozen comparison |
| --- | --- |
| A0 | Reproduce the #221 exact and #235 branch-preserving address controls unchanged as benchmarks. |
| A1 | Add top three script conversions per native-script name/locality component, with raw, Unicode NFC and digit-normalized originals retained. Retrieve by branch name or civic+street/locality combination. Keep distinct civic and unit numbers and contradictory effective addresses as vetoes. Retain results from both A0 and A1 for evaluation. |
| A2 | Re-score A1 using permitted #234 named geometry and its rival set, frozen before opening A1 results. For each point with an understood source, evaluate all eligible candidates at 25, 100 and 250 m as sensitivity radii, not accuracy estimates. Also evaluate all rivals inside the documented uncertainty bound if it exceeds 250 m. Treat missing precision, disputed vintage and copied pins as unresolved. Distinguish campus footprint from entrance. Report a nearest-point comparison for the geometry subset as a simple control. |

Check all 49 listings under all arms. Partition by usable script, authorized geometry, known origin and missing fields. Freeze all arms and offer the union of candidate pairs to two reviewers blinded to arm, with release and premise documentation independent of the scored evidence. Give reviewers the entire candidate group for each source listing so they can detect plausible rivals. Report actual labelled positives/negatives/unknowns, observed precision with denominator, per-arm coverage, newly reached listings and occurrences, and wrong-branch outcomes. Review every unique proposed promotion and up to 30 deterministic substitutions sampled without replacement by stable key from available same-chain/same-PIN different premises, distinct unit, shared grounds and shifted-pin cases. Keep controls separate from real-site rates and duplicate payloads together.

Advance only if A1 retrieves at least one additional independently verified site over A0 or A2 safely disambiguates one independently verified A1 rival group, with zero promoted wrong branches in adjudication or controls. Stop promotions on the first confirmed wrong branch. Stop the bounded test after one frozen pass if there are no eligible native-script pairs, no independent geometry/labels, or no marginal confirmed links. Preserve retrieval counts and request stable assessed premises ID/address for the unresolved cohort. Missing label coverage prevents accuracy claims. Planning cap is two analyst-days plus up to 30 paired control reviews. The team can run the eligible subset first and report the scope actually available.

### B. Branch and service episode alignment

**Question.** Does explicit time alignment preserve branch-specific historical support while rejecting pooled or impossible overlaps? Existing #232/#233 freeze original claims and modality/date categories. This comparison adds prospective decision-day intervals and tests what goes wrong if the same administrative site is carried forward across branches or dates. Run locally within each authorized source scope, then pool counts only. Start with all 12 AU3910 claim slots from #232 and 13 diagnostic MRI observation slots from #233. Attached historical operator notices and eligible equipment logs are comparator evidence, not additional primary slots. Keep zero-VF-link sites for source-level validation. The latest eligible source edition for each subject and the decision date are frozen before judging.

| Arm | Frozen comparison |
| --- | --- |
| B0 | Reproduce the owner source scope categories. Separately simulate a simple latest-document rule that uses an observed issuer name and modality to flag historical or current candidate wording. Record publication dates and pooled cases. No automatic service decision. |
| B1 | Require a physically host-specific crosswalk. Express branch identity validity and service or device events as separate intervals with explicitly missing bounds. For a historical claim require an event day or stated observation interval at the host, compatible with its premises episode. For a present-day claim require a dated, authorized operational observation whose stated effective coverage includes the decision date, or a branch-specific review under the shared freshness rules. Keep commissioning, operational events and repair apart. Never substitute publication or renewal date for service date. Treat pooled scope, stale or missing bounds, simultaneous branch ambiguity and opposing overlapping claims as explicit unresolved outcomes. |

Use two reviewers to independently annotate all 25 slots, blinding them to computed arm outcomes. Have them identify branch, date basis, unit, host and claim class from permitted documents before the crosswalk result is opened. Record event/branch coverage as both counts and fractions over 25, then historical-support precision among independently adjudicable promotions. Also report B0-to-B1 downgrades, newly resolved episodes, conflicting overlaps, eligible VF campus pairs, and the reconciled 30,264 national decisions. Use eight deterministic controls, a paired valid and invalid version for each of same-PIN host swap, relocation boundary, pooled-versus-branch activity, and reused device serial with a mismatched operating period. Require agreement or adjudication, retain unknowns as unknown, and report the number of independent documentary anchors. Two reviewers reading the same issuer report provide review agreement with one upstream source.

Advance B1 if it preserves at least one independently adjudicated historical site/service episode and either rejects a misattributed or temporally unsupported B0 claim or adds a correctly bounded episode, with zero erroneous promotions on reviewed slots or controls. Stop the test after all 25 slots and eight controls, or suspend promotions at the first wrong branch/time assignment. If there is no approved branch anchor or dated host-specific evidence, publish the gap and specify the exact missing date, physical host ID/address, period and procedure unit. Current service accuracy remains unmeasured until independent labelled outcomes exist. Planning cap is one analyst-day for interval comparison and two short reviews of the bounded slots, excluding clearance and document retrieval.
