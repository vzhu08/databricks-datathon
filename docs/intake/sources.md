# Sources for fictional intake warnings and care requests

The offline demo accepts scripted facts and structured answers. A positive in either layer stops ordinary questioning and hospital search, including when the other layer disagrees. Only an explicit negative resolves a warning as absent. An answer of unknown or conflicting, a missing answer, and unreadable text each remain visible. Five explicit negatives allow the remaining intake questions. They cannot rule out an emergency.

The table records the primary pages checked on November 21, 2026 (Eastern). Each link opens the publisher’s page. The section and review date identify the material used. Rule IDs use the form `intake.warning.<sign>.v1`. [The offline catalogue](../../app/intake/danger_signs.py) supplies their display text and source IDs.

## Limits for scenario authors

- Preserve a known warning before applying population limits or looking for a referral. Routine fictional hospital search covers the scripted adult population. Children, pregnancy and unresolved population context need explicit handling outside these five rules. None of the references validates a pediatric or obstetric decision pathway in the demo.
- A previous simulated trauma assessment is a project prerequisite. These sources provide no rule for assigning trauma care to every injury, chest concern or emergency. The visitor can confirm a completed summary only after the clinical prerequisite exists in the story.
- Use fixed narrative for display. Negated statements, remote events and recently resolved stroke signs have different meanings. Supply a structured answer only when the fictional facts establish what the question asks. Keep vague histories unknown.
- The source pages support bounded rationale. This review establishes no sensitivity, specificity, treatment guidance, destination, or service availability. The repository stores citations and original paraphrases. These references supply no national facility rows or service decisions.
