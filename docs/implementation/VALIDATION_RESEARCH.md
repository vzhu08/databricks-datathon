## Run an independent validation pilot

1. **Freeze the original population.** D or the authorized operator obtains the Virtue Foundation rows and postal mapping. For a Rajasthan slice, record the selection rule and source count, disputed state assignments, export time, input hash and one preserved ID per original row. Include records with missing coordinates or service fields. Verify row and ID preservation after each join. [Export procedure](../../databricks/enrichment/LOCAL_EXPORT.md)
2. **Reserve evidence before tuning.** Record each document’s publisher, underlying programme, date, checksum and intended role in a private manifest. Keep NQAS assessments and B’s reserved operational publication series away from feature design, thresholds and candidate adjudication until predictions are frozen. Check whether the original rows cite the same source. Mark overlaps or uncertain lineage and report them separately. For identity, a district-wise parliamentary ESI annexure offers a potential independent check on a limited population of scheme hospitals. Inspect its campus identifiers and coverage before adopting it. [Parliamentary ESI annexure](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/184/AU2671_LTWMZr.pdf?source=pqals)
3. **Draw two samples.** For the existing pilot limits of up to 50 identity pairs and 30 service claims, keep a seeded sample of eligible originals for estimating coverage and a separate challenge set for failure analysis. Include unmatched original rows so missed candidates can be found. Include rural or small facilities, shared names, conflicting addresses, expired evidence, and procurement/referral wording in the challenge set. Record eligibility, deduplication by campus group, selection probabilities where applicable, and a hash of the sampled IDs. Never pool the challenge set into a population rate. [Sprint limits](PLAN.md)
4. **Adjudicate the exact claim.** Two reviewers independently use held-out originals without seeing the proposed score or each other’s labels. For identity choose same campus, different campus or unresolved. For services record service name, onsite or referral, date, and supported, contradicted or unknown. For accreditation, review the named department and validity interval. For geography, report building plausibility and visibility. Preserve the document locator, evidence date, reviewer and reason, then reconcile disagreements. An absent entry stays unknown unless the source explicitly establishes the contrary.
5. **Publish a pilot report with denominators.** Show original rows preserved, candidate coverage, confirmed campus identities, unreviewed and unresolved cases, and independently supported service claims. Report correct confirmed campus decisions divided by adjudicated confirmations, with a binomial uncertainty interval where the sampling design allows it. Report missed candidates in the independently reviewed sample, and results separately for sites missing coordinates or absent from registries. Publish the disagreement count, dates, snapshot hashes and replay command. Keep personally identifying and restricted source material outside Git.
6. **Decide what can be shown.** Once a real pilot preserves IDs and has replayable reviewed campus links with independent documentary support and disclosed gaps, use it for a scoped demo. Show separate identity, location, certification and service results with dates and an explicit unknown state. Calibrate any proposed numeric score on newly held-out campuses after this pilot. A small audited sample has limited precision. Public data can support a named, dated claim. An assessment of overall clinical quality or real-time availability needs different evidence.

Each owner returns a manifest, aggregate counts, unresolved cases, permissible artifact location and exact replay instructions in their assigned issue. The integration lead reconciles record overlap and source-family reuse before updating this synthesis. Live Databricks publication and submission have separate acceptance checks.
